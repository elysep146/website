style="color:pink;">Figure collecting!

A hobby of mine is collecting figures! While many people have likely seen figures before, they don't always know the types of figures there are.

One of the most common type of figures are detailed Scale Figures!

Scale figures are usually very detailed, and are more expensive than other figures. These figures are also the ones many people enjoy collecting! These figures are not posable, come with an attached base, and are categorized by size. Sometimes these figures come with accessories that you have to assemble yourself. While not always the case, these figures tend to be made from Japanese manufacturers such as Bandai Namco and GoodSmile Company, and depict characters from Japanese media

Another common type of figure are Action figures!

Action figures typically are seen as children's toys, but they can also get pretty pricy, and aren't quite meant for children. While there are cheap toys for children, many adults collect action figures. The main purpose of Action Figures is to have good movement, and overall nice design. These figures are posable and do not come with a base. Almost all action figures are depictions of popular characters from superhero shows/movies and comics, heroes from the Marvel and DC franchises being most popular.

One of the pricier types of figures are Nendoroids!

Nendoroids are made exclusively by GoodSmile Company and are quite a bit smaller than other types of figures. They are categorized by the specific art style used when making them. Many people enjoy collecting Nendoroids due to the cute look, but even though they are small, the price is out of many peoples' budgets. Nendoroids can be created for many different characters and franchises, though typically GoodSmile Company uses characters from Japanese media. I wouldn't recommend these figures for beginner figure collectors.

The final type of figure I'll be demonstrating are Miniso figures!

Miniso figures don't really have a specific name, but their manufacture is the popular store Miniso! Miniso creates a wide variety of figures, and don't stick to one particular franchise. Miniso creates a lot of figures for the Sanrio/Hello Kitty franchise, as well as popular Japanese media and Zootopia. Since the recent release of Zootopia 2, Miniso has manufactured multiple types of surprise figures of the Zootopia characters, while in past years they focused on the Sanrio franchise. They seem to be using a mix of many franchises, and still creating figures of the same quality. Unlike other figures, these figures are usually on the lower end of pricing, and I would reccomend starting here for beginner collectors.

There are so many types of figures, but these are some of the more common types! While it wasn't too typical to collect figurines in the past, the rise in popularity of figurines grows with the new generation.
