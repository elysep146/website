The final type of figure I'll be demonstrating are Miniso figures!

Miniso figures don't really have a specific name, but their manufacture is the popular store Miniso! Miniso creates a wide variety of figures, and don't stick to one particular franchise. Miniso creates a lot of figures for the Sanrio/Hello Kitty franchise, as well as popular Japanese media and Zootopia. Since the recent release of Zootopia 2, Miniso has manufactured multiple types of surprise figures of the Zootopia characters, while in past years they focused on the Sanrio franchise. They seem to be using a mix of many franchises, and still creating figures of the same quality. Unlike other figures, these figures are usually on the lower end of pricing, and I would reccomend starting here for beginner collectors.
